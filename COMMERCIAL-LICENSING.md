# Commercial Licensing Guide

This document outlines the commercial licensing options, policy frameworks, and procurement pathways available for MARSHAL.

---

## 1. Overview

MARSHAL is available under two distinct licensing pathways:

1. **Community Edition**: Licensed under `AGPL-3.0-only`. Free for open-source use, subject to AGPL copyleft and network source-disclosure requirements.
2. **Commercial License**: A separate, proprietary license agreement granted directly by the copyright owner for organizations that require alternative licensing terms.

---

## 2. Commercial License Categories

The copyright owner offers tailored commercial license agreements across several engagement categories:

### Proprietary Integration
For organizations embedding or linking MARSHAL into proprietary internal tools or commercial software products without being subject to AGPL source-disclosure obligations.

### OEM Embedding & Redistribution
For hardware manufacturers, appliance providers, or software vendors distributing MARSHAL as an embedded component within proprietary commercial distributions.

### Enterprise Deployment
For large organizations requiring standardized enterprise licensing, central management rights, customized compliance terms, or private build authorization.

### Private & Custom Builds
For organizations requiring customized features, bespoke protocol extensions, or proprietary adapter integration built directly onto the MARSHAL runtime foundation.

### Managed Service & Cloud Hosting
For cloud providers or SaaS operators offering MARSHAL-based hosted agent runtime services to third parties.

### Commercial Support & Services
For organizations seeking formal support, maintenance, technical onboarding, or advisory service contracts alongside their licensing.

---

## 3. Requesting a Commercial License

To initiate a commercial licensing inquiry:

1. Identify your organization's deployment model (internal use, OEM distribution, SaaS, custom build).
2. Contact the copyright owner via email at `extreme29@proton.me` or via the official repository at [`https://github.com/Zen1th53/marshal`](https://github.com/Zen1th53/marshal).
3. The copyright owner will work with your legal and procurement teams to execute a tailored commercial license agreement.
