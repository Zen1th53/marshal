## How the core invariant is enforced

*Any qualified AI may think for MARSHAL. No AI may redefine MARSHAL.*

The guarantee rests on four mechanical properties, each covered by tests:

1. **Ranking is structural.** `constitution.Resolve` ranks claims only by
   `AuthorityLevel`. AI levels (8, 9) sit below every rule-bearing source, so a
   source cannot promote itself by how convincingly it describes itself.
2. **The advisory has no permission-bearing field.** `Advisory` carries
   `RecommendsApproval` but no field by which a model can state that approval
   is unnecessary. A model may always raise the bar and never lower it.
3. **Deterministic first, advisory second.** `Evaluate` reaches its verdict from
   `RuntimeState` before reading the advisory, then applies only the model's own
   *admissions* as escalations. Asserting compliance gains nothing.
4. **Malformed input is discarded, not fatal.** A hostile or broken advisory can
   neither command MARSHAL nor stall it; the decision proceeds deterministically.
