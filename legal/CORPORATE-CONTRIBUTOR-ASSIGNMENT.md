# Corporate Contributor Assignment Agreement (CCAA)

> **DRAFT — REQUIRES QUALIFIED SOFTWARE/IP LEGAL REVIEW BEFORE USE**

This Corporate Contributor Assignment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between the undersigned corporate entity ("Company") and `[COPYRIGHT OWNER NAME / DESIGNATED ENTITY]` ("Project Owner") regarding contributions made to the SLAVES project (`https://github.com/Zen1th53/slaves`).

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope

Where employees or contractors of Company create source code, documentation, or other materials for submission to SLAVES in the course of their employment or engagement, Company is the owner of the intellectual property rights in such submissions.

This Agreement establishes the legal assignment framework under which Company authorizes designated employees ("Authorized Contributors") to submit Contributions to SLAVES and assigns the underlying copyrights and patent rights to Project Owner.

---

## 2. Definitions

* **"Authorized Contributors"**: Employees or contractors of Company listed in **Schedule A** (or designated via Company's official corporate GitHub organization) who are authorized to submit Contributions on Company's behalf.
* **"Contribution"**: Any source code, documentation, bug fix, patch, test case, schema, design, or other material intentionally submitted by an Authorized Contributor for inclusion in SLAVES.

---

## 3. Corporate Copyright Assignment

1. **Assignment**: Company hereby assigns, transfers, and conveys to Project Owner all worldwide right, title, and interest, including all copyrights and copyright renewal rights, in and to all Contributions submitted by Authorized Contributors and accepted into SLAVES.
2. **Successor & Acquisition Transferability**: Company acknowledges and agrees that Project Owner may assign, transfer, sell, or license all assigned copyrights and project IP assets to any successor entity, affiliate, or acquiring company in connection with a corporate sale, merger, acquisition, or asset transfer **without requiring additional consent or notice to Company**.
3. **Licensing Authority**: Company acknowledges that Project Owner holds sole authority to dual-license accepted Contributions under open-source (`AGPL-3.0-only`), commercial, or proprietary terms.

---

## 4. Corporate Patent Grant

Company grants to Project Owner, and to recipients of software distributed by Project Owner, a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable patent license to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import, and otherwise transfer Contributions submitted by Company's Authorized Contributors.

This grant applies to all patent claims owned or licensable by Company or its Affiliates which are necessarily infringed by Company's Contributions alone or by combination of the Contributions with the SLAVES project.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> *Attorney Review Flag*: Scope of corporate patent grant and affiliate coverage must be reviewed by corporate IP counsel.

---

## 5. Company Authority & Representations

Company represents and warrants that:

1. It has full legal power and corporate authority to execute this Agreement and assign the IP rights described herein;
2. All listed Authorized Contributors have been duly authorized by Company to submit Contributions;
3. Contributions submitted by Authorized Contributors are original works of Company or its employees and do not knowingly infringe third-party patents, copyrights, trade secrets, or proprietary rights; and
4. Company will notify Project Owner in writing if an individual is no longer an Authorized Contributor.

---

## 6. Modification of Authorized Contributors & Revocation Scope

* **Schedule Updates**: Company may add or remove Authorized Contributors by submitting an updated **Schedule A** signed by an authorized corporate officer.
* **Effect of Revocation**: Revocation of an employee's authorization applies **prospectively only to future contributions**. Revocation does **not** invalidate, undo, or revoke copyright assignments or patent grants for Contributions submitted prior to written notice of revocation.
