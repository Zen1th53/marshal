## Stable contract

Evidence errors are typed and bounded, including invalid type, digest mismatch,
immutability, invalid edge/state, secret rejection and unavailable/stale
authorization. Durable evidence events contain bounded identifiers/digests and
do not persist raw provider output, fixture text or secret-bearing causes.

T06 is provider-neutral at the evidence boundary; provider metadata is treated
as data and does not provide authority.
